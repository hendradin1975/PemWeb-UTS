Education & Equality:
Two Supporting Goals

Good education only matters if everyone can reach it. That is why we focus on SDG 4 (Quality Education) and SDG 10 (Reduced Inequalities).

Getting to Know the SDGs

The SDGs are a global agreement to address shared challenges such as poverty, education, health, and the environment.

17 Goals,
One Agenda

The SDGs consist of 17 goals agreed upon by United Nations member states in 2015 and form part of the 2030 Agenda.

See all 17 Goals

Adopted by the UN General Assembly on 25 September 2015.

Our Focus

Of these 17 goals, we focus on the two most closely tied to inclusion: SDG 4 and SDG 10.

SDG 4
Quality Education

Ensure inclusive, equitable, and quality education, and promote lifelong learning opportunities for all.

Learn More

SDG 10
Reduced Inequalities

Reduce inequality within and among countries, and promote social, economic, and political inclusion for all, including persons with disabilities.

Learn More

Why are SDG 4 and SDG 10 connected?

Education opens opportunities, and equality ensures they reach everyone. Explore how both are put into practice through inclusive education.
